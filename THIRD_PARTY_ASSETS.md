# Third-party assets

The Furnace Engine head and crank models/textures in
`src/main/resources/assets/createfurnaceengine` are adapted copies of assets
from Create 6.0.1 for Minecraft 1.21.1.

- Source: https://github.com/Creators-of-Create/Create
- Authors: simibubi and the Create contributors
- License: MIT

The copied assets were renamed into the `createfurnaceengine` namespace so
they can be customized independently from Create.
